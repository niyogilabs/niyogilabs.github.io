About Niyogi Labs

Company Overview

Robotics & Automation Startup

Niyogi Labs is an early-stage technology startup focused on pioneering robotics, hardware automation, local-first IoT platforms, and hardware perception layers for AI agents.

The Name: 'Niyogi'

Etymology & Core Philosophy

In various Indian languages—including Hindi, Sanskrit & Marathi (नियोगी), Bengali / Bangla (নিয়োগী), Gujarati (નિયોગી), Telugu (నియోగి), Kannada (ನಿಯೋಗಿ), and Malayalam (നിയോഗി)—the word 'Niyogi' translates directly to "Delegate" or "One who is assigned a task or duty."

This concept embodies our founding vision. At Niyogi Labs, we build autonomous hardware systems, intelligent perception layers, and fail-safe automation tools designed to take over complex, repetitive, and critical tasks—allowing human engineers and teams to delegate with 100% confidence.
