Step 1 • The Problem

The Hidden Chaos of Household & Personal Deadlines

Every household quietly runs a complex legal, medical, and financial operation. Between your own paperwork, your spouse's certifications, your kids' school and healthcare milestones, and your aging parents' regular appointments, critical dates are scattered everywhere:

• High-Stakes Identification

Passports expiring right before a family holiday (or falling within the 6-month validity rule), and driver's licenses expiring unnoticed until you're at an airport gate or traffic stop.

• Health & Immunization Timelines

Pediatric vaccine booster schedules, annual pediatric vision/dental checkups, and senior prescription reviews easily fall through the cracks of busy workweeks.

• Vehicle & Property Compliance

Annual vehicle registration renewals, smog checks, auto insurance premium deadlines, and county property tax due dates that carry hefty late penalty surcharges.

Trying to manage this with sticky notes or separate personal reminder apps fails because family members don't share the same apps. But nearly everyone in the family has an email address and a default smartphone calendar (Google Calendar or Apple Calendar).
